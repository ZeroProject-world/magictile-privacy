Privacy Policy — Magic Tile Merge

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Magic Tile Merge ("the App") is a puzzle game developed by Jay Yang. This policy explains what information the App and its advertising partner collect, and how it is used.

Information collected by the App itself

The App does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. Your game scores are stored only on your device and are never sent anywhere.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect and use:

  • Device identifiers, including the Apple advertising identifier (IDFA) — only with your permission via the App Tracking Transparency prompt
  • Approximate (coarse) location inferred from your IP address
  • App usage and ad interaction data (impressions, clicks, diagnostics)

This data is used for third-party advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention, under Google's policies. Learn more at:
https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

You can decline tracking in the App's permission prompt or at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. The App remains fully playable if you decline; ads will simply be less personalized.

Children

The App is a general-audience puzzle game. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this page with a new "last updated" date.
